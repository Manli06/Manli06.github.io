About Me

An engineering mindset driven by experimentation, multiphysics simulations, and physical system design.

Electrical & Electronic Engineering Student

I am currently an Electrical and Electronic Engineering undergraduate student at the National Institute of Technology, Nagaland (NIT Nagaland), currently pursuing my 3rd Year (5th Semester).

My engineering curiosity revolves around understanding how electrical energy can be harvested, conditioned, and deployed efficiently. I am especially passionate about piezoelectric energy harvesting systems, exploring both physical transducer hardware (PZT, PVDF) and multiphysics finite element simulation in COMSOL Multiphysics.

I believe that true engineering excellence comes from a balanced synergy between theoretical mathematical modeling, computer simulations (MATLAB/Simulink, Python), and rigorous laboratory testing of electrical machines, transformers, and electronic circuits.

Practical Exposure

Laboratory Experience

Rigorous practical experiments conducted in the Electrical & Electronics Engineering laboratories at NIT Nagaland.

Electrical Measurements & AC Bridges

Maxwell Inductance Bridge

Measurement of self-inductance of a coil by comparison with a standard variable inductance.

Maxwell Inductance-Capacitance Bridge

Determination of unknown medium-Q inductance using a standard capacitor reference.

Wheatstone Bridge

High-accuracy measurement of unknown medium-range resistances under null-deflection galvanometer conditions.

Anderson Bridge

Precision measurement of self-inductance in terms of standard capacitance and resistances over a wide range.

Schering Bridge

Measurement of unknown capacitance, dielectric dissipation factor (tan δ), and dielectric loss angle.

Electrical Machines & Power Systems

DC Shunt Motor Speed Control

Speed regulation below and above rated RPM utilizing Armature Resistance Control and Field Flux Control methods.

Transformer Open-Circuit (OC) Test

Measurement of no-load current (Io), iron losses (Wi), and calculation of magnetizing branch parameters (Ro, Xm).

Transformer Short-Circuit (SC) Test

Evaluation of full-load copper losses (Wsc), equivalent resistance (Req), and leakage reactance (Xeq).

B-H Curve / Magnetic Hysteresis

Tracing magnetization characteristics of a ferromagnetic core using an oscilloscope to determine retentivity and coercivity.

Analog & Digital Electronics

PN Junction Diode Characteristics

Forward knee voltage (0.7V for Si) and reverse breakdown/saturation current profiling on breadboard.

Half-Wave Rectifier with π-Section Filter

Observation of output DC voltage, ripple factor attenuation, and capacitor-inductor filtering dynamics.

Half Subtractor & Full Subtractor

Truth-table realization of multi-bit arithmetic subtraction with borrow-in and borrow-out logic.

4-Bit Ripple Carry Adder

Cascaded full adders implementing parallel binary addition and analyzing ripple carry delay.

Multiplexer (4:1) & Demultiplexer (1:4)

Digital data routing, address line selection, and combinational function generator realization.

IC 7485 4-Bit Magnitude Comparator

Verification of magnitude comparison for 4-bit binary words under equality and inequality conditions.

Simulation & Computational Modeling

State-Space Modelling of Dynamic Systems

Deriving differential equations and constructing state matrices for RLC circuits and mechanical oscillators.

MATLAB / Simulink Response Experiments

Step and impulse responses, transfer function conversions, and pole-zero placement stability checks.

COMSOL Piezoelectric Cantilever Setup
